A cute and modern music player for YouTube Music and local music.

Listen to <b>YouTube Music</b> and music stored on your device, with live synced lyrics, music recognition, dynamic themes, listening statistics, extensive customization, Discord Rich Presence, widgets, and more.


<big><b>Features:</b></big>
<ul>
<li>Ad-free playback with background listening</li>
<li>Play local music files stored on your device</li>
<li>Access your YouTube playlists, liked songs, and subscriptions</li>
<li>Connect with your YouTube Music account</li>
<li>Fast startup and lightweight performance</li>
<li>Private and uninterrupted listening</li>
<li>Automatically balance loudness between songs with EBU R128 normalization</li>
<li>Adjust tempo, pitch, and playback speed</li>
<li>Smoothly crossfade between tracks</li>
<li>Use your device's system equalizer and spatial audio</li>
<li>View live synced lyrics while listening</li>
<li>Translate and romanize lyrics</li>
<li>Recognize songs playing around you</li>
<li>Track your listening statistics</li>
<li>Scrobble music to Last.fm</li>
<li>Sync your listening history with ListenBrainz</li>
<li>Show your currently playing music through Discord Rich Presence</li>
<li>Add music controls and information to your home screen with widgets</li>
<li>Enjoy a Material 3 interface with dynamic album-art colors</li>
<li>Responsive layouts for phones, tablets, and different screen sizes</li>
<li>Customize playback and interface behavior</li>
<li>Personalize gestures</li>
<li>Fine-tune animations and layouts</li>
</ul>
